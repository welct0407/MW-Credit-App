## Controls and recovery

- `partner_conversations_enabled`: independent turn and reply kill switch.
- `conversation_writes_enabled`: general API write switch; no effect on receipt posting authority.
- `partner_conversation_cutoff`: first activation timestamp; both event and capture time must be newer. Never reset it to replay history.
- `conversation_timeout_seconds`: 30–900 seconds, default 600.
- `mw-partner-conversation.timer`: 15 seconds after the previous cycle finishes, one turn per cycle so its reply is queued promptly; a process lock prevents overlap.

Generic processing/sending/writes remain false. Current master opt-in and English/ไทย preference control conversations; disabling the Partner blocks both reply channels. Fresh authenticated sender binding is also checked before email fallback. Receipt notification fallback retains its separate existing policy.

`partner-conversations.db` holds bindings, turns, tool calls and operation journals. `conversation-notices.db` holds replies and delivery attempts. Neither belongs in Git. An interrupted RUNNING turn becomes INTERRUPTED and never calls `turn/start` again. READY answers can be queued after restart without re-executing the turn. Unknown operations retain the request and immutable final-state checks, and block all further conversation writes until reconciled. Reconcile read-only against the owning app and affected related records; never erase a reservation or retry a possible write. Identical Add payloads are also blocked across new messages to prevent accidental duplicate entry. An intentional repeated identical entry needs operator reconciliation. Multi-step operations are not atomic; successful earlier steps remain recorded if a later step fails. A failed turn gets a localized limitation reply when identity and delivery controls permit it.

Unknown sends never retry. Definite unsent email rejection retries at most three total attempts with backoff. A reply over 1,000 characters goes as one complete email; no partial Instagram response is sent first. Provider acceptance is recorded accurately and does not prove inbox delivery.
