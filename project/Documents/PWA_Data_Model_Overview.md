# PWA data model

Current native ownership, 14 October 2026. Exact definitions belong in the [dictionary](Data%20Dictionary/README.md) and immutable MW-Credit-App migrations. This is a source/evidence reconciliation, not a new live capture.

| Domain | Grain and relationships |

| Cash | Holders own accounts; source transactions produce ledger effects; openings/transfers preserve reconciliation. | Partners | Sign-in/roles, contributions and settlements; delivery preference is separate from identity |
| Expenses | Obligations/cash effects; linked reimbursements remain independent where defined. R140 (V142, Open): a manual expense can be pending payment. Pending is derived, not stored: Source Type `Manual`, Amount above 0, `"Ref Paid By Cash Holder"` and `"Ref Paid By Cash Account"` both null and no `r005_cash_cutover_sources` row (those historical rows carry no payer by design); it writes no Cash Ledger row. Paying it is an edit with a paying account (the System ledger row is dated on the Expense Date, not the pay date); clearing the account returns it to pending and removes the row. A manual expense's cash movement is dated on its paid date (V144, nullable `"Business Expenses"."Paid Date"`, optional command key `paidDate` with a paying account, not after today; null means the Expense Date; reads carry `paidDate`, `storedPaidDate`, and null while pending); a referral rebate keeps its own dates (a V3 rebate moves cash on the settlement date, the offset part on the close date; legacy V1 and V2 paid-at-close rows are dated on the close date and their real pay dates are not stored). The payee of a manual expense can be an existing borrower (a referrer or anyone else; V143, `"Ref Payee Borrower"` with the borrower's name kept in `"Payee Name"`, optional command key `payeeBorrowerId`) or a typed name such as Dad; the link never fills Related Borrower, and a referral rebate keeps its referrer as payee. Borrower details carry `expensesOwedAmount` and `expensesOwedCount` (pending manual expenses paid to that borrower, separate from rebates owed) and borrower search carries `isReferrer`. Expense records carry `paymentState` ('pending' or null) and `pendingAmount`; the cash position carries `expensesPayable` (amount and count). R111: a referral rebate expense can be settled by offset: the nullable "Ref Offset Payment" links it to a payment on the referrer's loan (method "Referral Rebate Offset", id `ro122:<closing payment>[:<loan>]`: from V131 the default offset appends the closed loan, so two loans closed by one payment get two offsets; the Loan Close command path and rows already posted keep `ro122:<closing payment>`), Lisa's cash nets to zero that day, and deleting the closing payment removes both; from V124 any close of a referred loan today (Receive payment, agent, chat) offsets the same way. R129 (Released to DEV and PROD on 5 November 2026, V137): new rows carry Rule Version `REFERRAL_REBATE_V3`, meaning the cash moves on settlement, not at close; V1 and V2 rows keep paid-at-close. Pending is derived, not stored: V3, `"Ref Rebate Settlement"` null and Amount above the offset amount (pending = Amount − offset amount); the nullable `"Ref Rebate Settlement"` links a paid rebate to `pwa_rebate_settlements` (immutable settlement rows: referrer, date, whole-baht amount, Lisa's account, method, optional bank reference; created and deleted only by `rebate-settlement.create` and `.delete`). Cash rule: a V3 expense projects a ledger movement only for its offset amount (none without an offset), so Lisa nets to zero on an offset day, and the settlement projects one `Rebate Settlement` movement for the sum of the rebates it pays. The expense keeps its amount and close date, so profit and the Partner split are unchanged. Deleting a closing payment whose rebate is paid is refused until the settlement is deleted |
| Analytics | Portfolio/date and account/date snapshots plus live SQL views. R118 (Released to DEV and PROD on 3 November 2026): a paid late charge is profit on its payment date like interest; profit relations sum `"Interest Paid" + "Late Charge Paid"`, `olap_repayments_analytics."Interest Paid"` stays interest-only, and `"Total Interest Received"` / `"Total Interest Earned"` fold late in (no new loan or borrower column). The referral rebate basis becomes interest plus late charge received: min(round(10% x (interest + late charge received)), 1,000). R120 (Released to DEV and PROD on 4 November 2026; V135): the 10% and 1,000 are the defaults of the referrer's optional terms, the nullable `Borrowers."Referral Rebate Rate"` (whole percent 0 to 100) and `Borrowers."Referral Rebate Cap"` (whole baht, 1 or more); the rule becomes min(round((interest + late charge received) x rate / 100), cap), read when the close posts, and the applied terms are stored on the expense as the nullable `"Rebate Rate"` and `"Rebate Cap"` (Rule Version `REFERRAL_REBATE_V2`; earlier rows are null) The income statement views append a "Late charges received" memo column; Dashboard, Cash and Management Analytics tiles are unchanged |
| Assessments | Stored inputs/results with freshness checks and nullable borrower linkage |

Authorized source CRUD is supported where SQL reconciles allocations, repayments, cash and ownership. Preserve later unrelated transactions/journals. Do not impose a new reversal framework or regenerate separately recorded historical charges.

Ordinary CRUD remains the accepted correction path (V59/V60 removed the older posted-amount guard). The command request-id journal and payload hash are enforced only on the Node command path, not on direct SQL writes. Since V93 (R064) the system cash-ledger rows are written only by the source triggers through the definer function `sync_cash_ledger`; the application and reviewer roles hold no direct write on "Cash Ledger", and manual cash movements change only through the governed command path. Direct source deletes by the application role are not a supported correction path. `Test-AccessMatrix.sql` asserts the migration-owned grants, so grant drift fails database CI.

Original daily interest uses frozen integer percentage points. Daily-interest principal changes use the governed original rate; fixed/installment schedules retain their rules. Unknown original terms/opening balances remain unknown.

## Native service records

The command journal stores idempotent request identity/outcome. PWA and machine entry points authenticate separately and converge on the native command boundary. Agent receipt commands supply deterministic request IDs and validated archive evidence. See [PWA/API contracts](Data%20Dictionary/systems/pwa/README.md).

Notification events, deliveries, subscriptions and preferences separate committed events from transport retries. Job runs record duplicate-safe scheduled work. These records are not additional financial receipts. The push text is derived at send time from the event and its source rows (V99 `pwa_notify_content_v1`, R080); no content is stored in these tables. The Notifications history text (R089, V103 `pwa_notify_history_content_v1`) is derived the same way for the rows of one history page and is not stored either.

## Receipts

"Payments" carries 25 triggers of its own (besides foreign-key constraint triggers). They fire in trigger-name order, so the names (`a000_` first through `zzzzzz_` last) are the execution order: do not rename or add one without updating the firing-order test. The list is in [payments.yaml](Data%20Dictionary/systems/postgresql/relations/payments.yaml) and the snapshot in `scripts/database/Test-TriggerOrder.sql`.

Payments retain manual-upload and agent evidence fields. Both use the private GCS bucket with their own validated object conventions. Manual replacement does not enqueue agent processing. Existing native references are resolved from verified mappings, never guessed filenames.

Access requires authenticated ownership/permission checks. Preserve object generations, hashes and required SQL identifiers even when historical names contain “AppSheet”.

## User flows and ownership

R116 splits principal write-offs from profit on the Dashboard and in the income statement: a write-off is the Principal Paid of the `df10:` loss-pair repayments on their Payment Date; Gross Profit is the Interest Paid of non-`df10:` repayments (the former Operating Income); Net Profit = Gross Profit - Expenses - Write-offs, unchanged in value. Realized, Today's, Yesterday's and Total Profit are gross, and past months restate with gross higher by the write-off. Unsettled Profit, partner shares and cash reconciliation are unchanged. The lifetime figure is the ledger sum of write-off postings (V129 "Principal Write-offs" on the portfolio summary). From V130 a write-off is a `df10:` posting or any zero-cash loss pair (principal X, interest -X, on a non-`lc7:`/`ls2:` charge with exactly one repayment), so the two AppSheet-era loans (6,000 in August) count as write-offs and the figure equals "Total Principal Loss". Operational pages cover Dashboard, Collection, Borrowers, Loans, Charges, Payments, Expenses and cash statements. Management covers partners, holders/accounts, contributions, settlements, cash movements, openings, assessments and analytics/validation. Server capabilities and Partner roles control access.
