This is the existing standard consent flow, not an expiresWithSession exception. [RFC6749§3.1](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6749#section-3.1) permits the endpoint query and requires clients to retain it when adding parameters. The locked provider rejects duplicate prompt/scope parameters; conflicting/overwritten consent never creates offline authority. Explicit same-owner browser approval remains mandatory. No request scope or prompt is silently changed by the server.
