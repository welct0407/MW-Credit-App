## Changes
- Evidence (agent): Forge's read of PROD since 14:04Z: every Instagram listing failure (1 of 6 summariser 25 x 100 listings and 2 of 186 poll 100 x 20 listings) was a non-4xx `InstagramAPIError` at 30.5 to 30.7 s, while successes took at most 9.1 s; timings scale with the number of conversations per page; the owner's refresh tap for the Gun group at 17:39Z failed this way and was recorded only as `GROUP_SUMMARY_FAILED`; the `first_page` counters stayed 0.
- Agent: (1) The summary cycle's Instagram client uses a 15 s read timeout (`SUMMARY_READ_TIMEOUT`); the poll, `--mark-all` and `--dry-run` keep 40 s. (2) One retry per run on a response of 500 or above or a transport failure, never on a 4xx, and only within the run budget; the effect gains `retries`. (3) First-page counting: `skipped` is counted before the request, and the new `failed` counts a first-page request that failed. (4) Fixed error codes `INSTAGRAM_HTTP_<status>[_<graph code>]`, `INSTAGRAM_TIMEOUT` and `INSTAGRAM_UNREACHABLE` replace the bare `GROUP_SUMMARY_FAILED` for Instagram failures; the dashboard shows them as "Other error" until a dashboard batch adds the words (carried over). A lighter listing (fewer messages per conversation) was considered and dropped, because the timings scale with conversations per page, not messages. No unit, setting, secret or table change. Catalogue revision 44, services unchanged; `R171_REVISIONS` = (43, 44).
- PWA part (owner confirmed in this session; evidence: the owner's troubleshooting file, Windows desktop PWA at R165, a focus refresh of 159 s of which about 100 s was the hidden pause): on a computer (`chatDevice()` returns `computer`) the offline-dataset sync keeps running while the window is hidden, as if visible; phones and tablets are unchanged. Also: the midnight full re-download is kept and now also runs hidden on a computer, and no 200 ms timer runs between chunks while hidden. UX-HSYNC decisions: UX-HSYNC-01: a hidden sync is background: no progress bar, no "Syncing…", no live-region message; only "Updated HH:MM" moves (no new mechanism; pinned by tests). UX-HSYNC-02: returning mid-sync lets it finish quietly; nothing is announced on return (no new mechanism; pinned by tests). UX-HSYNC-03: dropped. UX-HSYNC-03 was dropped after the first CI run on PR #353 (run 44146458061, head `f1f3014`) failed e2e shard 1 on desktop Chromium (6 specs plus 1 flaky): skipping the forced refresh within 30 s of the last check on a computer broke the established rule that every focus refreshes at once. Decision: that rule stays on all devices; `focused()` and `visible()` reset back-off and check every domain as before R171, and `visible()` still re-arms the quiet-retry hold. UX-HSYNC-04: the stale label ("Saved data from HH:MM") re-renders immediately on becoming visible, on all devices (display only). UX-HSYNC-05: the R163 quiet retry is unchanged while hidden; holds and quiet state are not cleared on return; an overdue hold fires on return; a task in hold publishes no failure; a persistent failure shows the existing header text until success. UX-HSYNC-06: toasts shown while hidden are not held; shipped as is (the warning icon persists). Accepted: Chrome throttles hidden timers, so the 30 s poll can stretch to about 1 minute after 5 minutes hidden, and a desktop left open keeps polling the read service. Carried over (not fixed here): receipts answer 409 `selection_changed` at 00:01 Bangkok. PWA Hosting only: no server, SQL or offline-dataset shape change.
- Byte's deviation: `instagram.py` gains `InstagramUnavailable` (a `ConfigurationError` subclass with `timed_out` and an identical message) so transport failures map to codes; six test harnesses were given the new import as a phone.
