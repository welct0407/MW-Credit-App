# Receipt charge classification — 25 September 2026

Cause: closure classification preceded ordinary charge eligibility, and any receipt at least as large as any open loan's principal was treated as a closure even without an exact payoff match. This misclassified borrowers with several loans and a small outstanding principal on one loan.

Only outside that limit, check exact same-day daily-interest payoff candidates; one remains manual closure, multiple remain an ambiguous hold, and no exact match becomes reviewer-approved overpayment.
