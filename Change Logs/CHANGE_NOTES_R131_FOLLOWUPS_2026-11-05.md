- **(e) List return position (owner request, 6 November 2026).** Owner: "Run through all the pages to check the consistency of behavior: if I click an item in a list to open another page, then click the go back button, it should take me to the same position in the list." Scope: PWA Hosting only; no SQL, server, infrastructure, data or deployment. Status: implemented and checked locally (Byte, Chase); Echo's UX pass and the pull request checks pending. Design: one shared helper `apps/pwa/src/list-return.ts` (`useListReturn()` returning leave and restore). On leaving a list it captures the window `scrollY`, the `scrollTop` of every scrolling ancestor and the opened row's `data-record-id`; on Back it restores over a rAF loop of at most 500 ms until the row reappears, then focuses it, and stops at once on user input. Swipe-back inherits Back. Implementation: `useListReturn()` exposes `leave(target, {top?, keep?})`, `restore` and `cancel`; `top` also resets the window (this fixed the mobile Expenses defect Chase found at scrollY 901). Changed files: `apps/pwa/src/list-return.ts` (new), `tests/unit/list-return.test.mjs` (new, 10 tests), `tests/e2e/list-return-independent.spec.ts` and `tests/e2e/fixtures/list-return-harness.tsx` (new, Chase); edited `SharedBorrowerDetail`, `BorrowerPayments`, `LoanRecords`, `PaymentGroups` and `CollectionReceipts` (`data-record-id` only), `LoanRelated` and `LoanRecord` (related page and cursors kept in a `LoanRecord` ref keyed by loan id), `ChargeRecord`, `CollectionRecords`, `ExpenseRecords`, `ReferrerRecords` (the old `listScroll` and window `scrollTo` replaced by the hook), `StandaloneUpcoming`, `CashWorkspace`, `ManagementWorkspace`, `ManagementFinancialWorkspace`, `OfflinePayments` and `live-main` (Notifications only). Beyond the design, Byte anchors the tapped row to its screen position while content above it loads, leaves alone a list that stayed visible and was scrolled by the user, and focuses a disabled (busy) row once it is enabled. Side effects: the first Referrers list load no longer forces the window to the top, and Upcoming Charges details now open at the top on a phone. Known limitations: on a wide desktop (above 1199 px) the Management workspace and Notifications lists do not scroll (`main` is `overflow:hidden`, no scroll container), so there is no position to restore (existing behavior, not changed here); no new spec covers Management, Cash, Saved changes or Notifications (Notifications needs `VITE_NOTIFICATIONS`), only the existing specs not regressing; no WebKit run and no real-device swipe (Chromium CDP touch only). No financial or authorization behavior changed. Behavior: Back from a page opened from a list returns to the same scroll position with the opened row in view and focused; where the row has gone (deleted or filtered out) the position is restored as far as the list allows and nothing errors.
