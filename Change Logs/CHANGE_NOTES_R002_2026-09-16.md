## Implementation and checks

Implemented in Dev: Flyway **V18**, OLTP **1.000098**, OLAP **1.000096**. Production untouched. V17 completed in 103 ms, V18 in 175 ms; Flyway validate passed. Applied V17/V18 are immutable. Owner explicitly clarified that the safeguard must warn at form level and requested a one-row SQL view containing limit and profit-share ratios. That is the final design.

- New loan gross principal must be positive and no greater than signed contributed capital minus outstanding principal. Contribution rows add; other transaction types subtract, matching existing Total Cash Pool semantics. All dates and borrowers count. Outstanding principal uses canonical Loans principal less linked Repayments principal paid, including earlier rows in the same transaction; retained interest/profit, expenses and settlements do not increase capacity. The gross amount is checked before first-day netoff.
- SQL guards every new Loans insert, including sync/API writes. A shared advisory try-lock covers Loans, Repayments and Cash Pool Contributions writes; contention fails promptly with a retry message. READ COMMITTED is required. Existing loan edits and withdrawals can cause a deficit; subsequent new loans are blocked. This is a new-loan safeguard, not a universal capital-adequacy constraint.
- V18 creates read-only SQL view **Loan Form Context**: one `cashpool` Text key row with Contributed Capital, Outstanding Principal, Available Cashpool and Partner A/B Profit Share. Three amounts are Price/THB(2), ratios Percent(2); no initial/App formulas. The current all-date ratios mirror Current Pool Share and do not replace historical repayment/expense allocation. OLTP imports only this summary as READ_ONLY on database-3, with no filter or menu/edit workflow. The temporary contributions app import was removed; the underlying ledger was never changed. OLAP retains ownership and its existing contribution configuration. No new base table/column or backfill.
