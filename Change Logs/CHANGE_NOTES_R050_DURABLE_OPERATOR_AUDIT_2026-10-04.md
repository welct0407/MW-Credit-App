# R050 — Durable operator intent and truthful relay health

Status: implemented and tested in isolated Cloud PostgreSQL; not deployed to DEV or PROD. R050 remains Open. V57 and the deployed retrospective recovery remain unchanged. No production financial request was executed during this development task.

The supported Cloud operator executor prepares canonical central intent, commits it and independently reads it back before opening the business write connection. V58 adds the audited entry point and disables the legacy six-argument entry point. The source marker hashes the exact canonical UTF-8 text. Duplicate operations and uncertain payment scopes prohibit another dispatch. Transport failure is observed through the source marker and never automatically retried. PostgreSQL administrators remain trusted: the SQL function cannot independently prove a commit in another database; the supported executor enforces that order.

The proposed relay inspector uses the latest completed job, running age and pending source markers. A current running process cannot erase the last completed failure. Missing completion or inspection failure is DOWN; success older than 180 seconds, a run older than 180 seconds, or backlog older than 180 seconds is DEGRADED. Missing central intents older than 30 seconds are DOWN; at most 20 oldest candidates are inspected per sample. Existing component labels, numeric status meanings and Grafana rule identities are retained. The collector records bounded numeric evidence only.
