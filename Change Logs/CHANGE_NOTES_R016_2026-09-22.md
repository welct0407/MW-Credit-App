Terminology clarification: V20/V23 already derive `Reimbursement / Advance Due` from negative recorded balances; V34 exposes the shorter `Advance Due`, and V35 reconstructs it by date. The new label **Partner advances** overstates the evidence: the metric is the magnitude of negative account balances, not proof of a partner loan or reimbursement liability. At account grain, Business Cash Held = max(balance, 0), Advance Due = max(-balance, 0); net cash sums signed balances. Reconciliation difference was introduced in R016 reporting (V34/V35), not as an operational transaction: V35 calculates signed net custody minus remaining capital minus cumulative retained net profit. Adding that residual makes a bridge balance by construction; it does not independently prove the records correct. Selection Status describes valid continuous date coverage; Cash Status describes historical availability and whether that arithmetic residual is effectively zero. No terminology or diagnostic visuals were changed during this explanation request.
